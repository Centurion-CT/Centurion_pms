Centurion platform: sign-up and console

The pages that turn Centurion into software other apartment and hotel businesses can sign up for. They use the same fonts, navy and blue as the staff sign-in and desk app, so going from sign-up to the dashboard feels like one product. The thread through all of them is the business's own web address.

Decisions these pages assume

  • The platform address is centurionpms.com. Businesses get name.centurionpms.com, and Centurion Apartments keeps centurionapartments.ng.
  • Short names like www, app, admin, console and login are reserved.
  • Sign-up creates the business, its first branch and the manager in one go (the existing /setup logic).
  • The console lives at console.centurionpms.com and is for you and anyone you add. It has no "log in as this business" button. That can be added later with an audit log if you need it.
  • Pausing blocks staff logins and online bookings but deletes nothing.

Copy to confirm before launch

  • Pricing is marked "to be decided" on the home page. The suggested wording promises free early access and 60 days' notice.
  • "We'll load your bookings from a spreadsheet" promises hands-on help.
  • "Transfers are matched to the right booking" and "Export your bookings and guests whenever you want": check these work as described today.
  • The Centurion Apartments quote is written for them. They need to approve it.
  • Console businesses, numbers and owners are sample data. The desk screenshots are the approved desk-app mockups.
