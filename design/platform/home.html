Questions

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. You start on your-business.centurionpms.com. When you're ready, add your own domain in Settings and we'll show you the one DNS record to set. Both keep working.

I have more than one building. Is that extra work?

No. Add each building as a branch. Staff can work in one branch or several, and you see all of them together.

Can other businesses on Centurion see my guests?

No. Every business has its own address, staff and data. Nobody outside your business can see your bookings, guests or money.

What does it cost?

Pricing to be decided. Suggested wording: “Free while we're in early access. We'll give you 60 days' notice before any charge.”

Can I bring my bookings from another system?

Yes. Send us a spreadsheet of your upcoming bookings and guests and we'll load them before your first day.
