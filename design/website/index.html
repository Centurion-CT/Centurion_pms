centurionapartments.ng: three directions

Each option has a home page (availability finder plus location explorer) and a results page that searches all five Abuja buildings at once and starts a booking. Click through: the location picker, map pins, filters and booking steps work. Prices and locations are real (from the current Tripmata page). Free-room counts are sample data. Photos are stand-ins until we have a proper shoot.

A · Availability Index

Data-led, built on the logo

The search is the headline: "I need a place in [any of our 5 locations] from [date] to [date], for [2 guests]." Next to it, the logo's bars become live bars, one per building, sized by how many apartments are free tonight.

  • The closest fit to the brand: blue, precise, a bit like a booking tool.
  • Map and list explorer, plus a table showing which apartment types each building has.
  • Results are grouped by building, with a stay summary that follows you down the page.

B · Neighbourhoods

Editorial and hotel-like, closest to Diplomat Enclave's feel

Full-bleed photography with the five location names set large in Bodoni as the headline. Hover a name and the photo behind changes to that building. The booking bar docks under the hero, like Diplomat's, but adds a location picker.

  • The most "premium hotel" of the three, and the most dependent on good photos.
  • Each neighbourhood is sold on its area: Wuse for nightlife, Zone 4 for business, Jabi for the lake.
  • Results read like a brochure. Booking opens in a side drawer.

C · Map Desk v2, rebuilt on a design system

App-like: map-first search, like Sonder or Airbnb

A real map of Abuja with a price marker on each building, beside a location list that stays in step with the map. Every listing has an availability strip: 14 bars, one per night, taken from the logo. Clicking a listing opens a detail panel with a calendar, then a checkout step.

  • One stylesheet of tokens and components, shown on its own page.
  • Blue only for actions; green and amber only for availability.
  • Three screens: home, search results with detail, and checkout.

Mixing is fine

The parts can be combined, for example B's hero and neighbourhood stories with A's free-tonight bars, or C's results page under either home page. The research notes and the plan for connecting to the HMS are in README.md in this folder.
